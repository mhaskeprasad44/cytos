Project: 90° Rotary Pneumatic Fixture for Automotive Frame Welding
AUTOMOTIVE TIER-1 COMPONENT FABRICATION

90° Pneumatic Weld-Indexing Fixture for Robot Welding Cell

The Challenge: An automotive supplier in Chakan, Pune was experiencing high weld porosity and spatter defects because the welding robot had to reach awkward vertical angles to weld an L-shaped structural bracket. The manual clamping setup also took 95 seconds per part.

The CyTOS Solution: We designed a custom heavy-duty fixture equipped with pneumatic toggle clamps and a 90° pneumatic rotary actuator. After loading, the fixture clamps the workpiece securely and rotates the joint by 90 degrees into a flat horizontal orientation, allowing the robot torch to weld downhand at maximum travel speed.

Project: Turnkey Robotic Adhesive Dispensing & Component Placement
AUTOMOTIVE SUNROOF ASSEMBLY LINE

Automated Hot-Adhesive Dispensing & Component Placement Cell

The Challenge: Manual silicone/hot-melt glue application on panoramic sunroof reinforcement brackets caused uneven bead thickness, adhesive runoff, and high scrap rates under water-ingress testing.

The CyTOS Solution: CyTOS delivered a turnkey 4-axis motion station with closed-loop pressure-controlled dispensing nozzles, automatic vision-guided part localization, and vacuum transfer pick-and-place arms. The machine meters adhesive down to ±0.05 grams and inspects bead continuity in-line.
