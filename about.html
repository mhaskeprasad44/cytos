About CyTOS (Cycle Time Optimising Solutions)

We are a Pune-based engineering and precision manufacturing company. Built upon foundational mechanical calculations, in-house motion controller hardware, and rigorous quality standards, we partner with manufacturers across India to drive down cycle times and elevate throughput.

THE CYTOS DIFFERENCE

Why Engineering Leaders Trust CyTOS

We reject the prevailing market practice of assembling lightweight imported kits with generic software.

1. Structural Safety Factor 2.0
FEA Simulated Rigidity

Every gantry, frame, and bed is designed with finite element analysis (FEA) to ensure a dynamic safety factor of 2.0. This guarantees zero frame deflection under maximum lateral cutting feeds and suppresses harmonics that cause premature tool wear.

2. In-House Controller Cores
No Black-Box Dependency

Unlike competitors who rely on unserviceable third-party breakout boards, CyTOS designs its own 32-bit DSP and FPGA motion controller hardware. We possess 100% control over firmware, kinematic algorithms, and custom PLC ladder logic.

3. True 24x7 Duty Rating
Industrial Component Sizing

Every bearing, guide rail (HIWIN / THK), drive servo (Delta / Yaskawa), and electrical relay is oversized to run 3 shifts continuously without thermal throttling or loss of positional repeatability.

4. Laser-Interferometer QA
Sub-Micron Calibration

Before leaving our Pune factory, every axis is laser-calibrated across its entire travel stroke to compensate for pitch error and backlash. Dynamic spindle runout is verified under 3 microns with precision dial indicators.

5. Turnkey Single-Partner Delivery
From Concept to Site Training

We don't just ship a wooden crate to your gate. CyTOS engineers supervise unloading, machine leveling, electrical connection, sample part signoff, and comprehensive operator and maintenance crew training at your plant.

6. Guaranteed Spare Parts Support
Pune Local Inventory

Because we manufacture in Pune, critical spare parts (spindles, collets, sensors, drive boards) are stocked right at our factory. No waiting 6 to 10 weeks for overseas shipments while your production line sits idle.
