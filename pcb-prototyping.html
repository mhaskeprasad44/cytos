PCB Rapid Prototyping & Educational CNC

Eliminate prototype turnaround delays and hazardous chemical etching. Convert Gerber design files into functional single and double-sided circuit boards in 30 minutes right in your laboratory, complete with automated depth-leveling and safe acrylic enclosure.

In brief: CyTOS PCB prototyping machines (PCBE3020 and CNC 3030 series) provide fast, chemical-free isolation milling, through-hole drilling, and edge contour routing for R&D engineering labs and university maker spaces. Equipping Tomorrow's Engineers Across India

Traditional PCB prototyping in academic labs often relies on messy chemical etching baths with toxic ferric chloride fumes and long processing times. The CyTOS PCBE3020 changes that completely: students design their schematic in KiCad or Eagle, export Gerber files, and watch their board milled mechanically in minutes inside a secure, interlocked cabinet.

  • Curriculum Ready: Includes ready-to-run lab exercises, student manuals, and sample projects.
  • Interlocked Safety: Spindle automatically cuts power if the transparent enclosure door is opened during operation.
  • Clean Lab Operation: Integrated HEPA vacuum suction nozzle captures 99.8% of copper dust.
