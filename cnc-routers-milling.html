BUILT FOR INDUSTRIAL WORKSHOPS

Zero Gantry Flex. Maximum Chip Load.

Many commercial routers cut corners with lightweight aluminum extrusions that shudder under lateral cutting loads, causing chatter marks, ruined end mills, and inaccurate parts. CyTOS machines are constructed from heavy, stress-relieved steel rectangular hollow sections (RHS) with ground gantry pads and precision HIWIN linear guideways.

  • Dual Y-Axis Synchronized Servos: Zero rack twisting across wide 8-foot gantries.
  • Oil-Mist Micro Lubrication: Flushes chips cleanly when cutting sticky aerospace-grade 6061 aluminium.
  • High-Flow Vacuum Clamping: Multi-zone vacuum bed with matrix gasket channels holds thin sheets dead flat without mechanical clamps.
