ENGINEERING FAQS

Robotic Dispensing Automation FAQs

Common technical questions on fluid viscosity, pump selection, and cycle times answered directly by CyTOS applications engineers.

How does CyTOS prevent adhesive stringing and dripping at the end of a dispensing cycle?

Every CyTOS dispensing cell incorporates a programmable suck-back valve or reverse progressive cavity rotor rotation at the exact millisecond dispensing halts. This breaks fluid surface tension cleanly at the nozzle tip, preventing tailing, dripping, or unintended contamination of adjacent workpiece surfaces.

Can CyTOS robotic dispensing cells handle 2-component (2K) polyurethanes and epoxies?

Yes. We integrate synchronized dual-rotor progressive cavity dosing pumps or dynamic gear pumps with disposable static mixing nozzles. The mixing ratio is mathematically controlled by servo motor gear ratios within ±1% volumetric accuracy, preventing improper curing or resin-rich streaks.

What is the delivery and on-site commissioning lead time across India?

Standard Cartesian gantry dispensing cells typically ship in 6 to 8 weeks from 3D design signoff. Custom articulated robot cells with vision inspection ship in 8 to 12 weeks.
