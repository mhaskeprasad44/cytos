Why Indian Fabricators Rely on the CyTOS VDM Series

Designed from the ground up for continuous industrial duty, heavy metal chip loads, and maximum cycle time reduction.

⚙️

Simultaneous Multi-Spindle Output

Configured with dual or triple synchronized drilling/milling heads. Produce 2 to 3 identical electrical panel cutouts or hole patterns in a single pass, slashing production cycle times by up to 65%.

💪

High-Torque BT30 / BT40 Spindles

Unlike high-speed routing spindles, VDM spindles are engineered for low-end torque (100 to 6,000 RPM) allowing rigid drilling up to 32 mm diameter and thread tapping up to M16 in solid mild steel.

🛡️

Heavy Damped Cast Iron Bed

Heavy stress-relieved Meehanite grade cast iron bed ensures high vibration absorption during interrupted cuts, delivering mirror surface finishes and preventing tool chatter or premature carbide chipping.
