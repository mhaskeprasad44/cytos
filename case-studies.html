CASE #01 • AUTOMOTIVE TIER-1 ASSEMBLY

Automotive Panoramic Sunroof Hot-Adhesive Dispensing & Component Placement

The Initial Bottleneck: An automotive supplier in Chakan, Pune was assembling panoramic sunroof reinforcement brackets manually. Operators used manual glue guns to apply sealant around complex perimeter channels. Human inconsistency led to frequent bead starvation, adhesive overflow, and a 5.8% reject rate during hydrostatic pressure testing. The manual process took 145 seconds per assembly.

The CyTOS Engineering Solution: CyTOS designed and manufactured a turnkey 4-axis Cartesian automation station. The system integrates heated positive-displacement gear pumps, closed-loop pressure regulation, and a synchronized pneumatic pick-and-place gantry. Workpiece location is verified optically prior to nozzle motion.

CASE #02 • ROBOTIC WELDING CELLS

90° Pneumatic Weld-Indexing Fixture for Perpendicular Seam Welding

The Initial Bottleneck: A commercial vehicle component manufacturer had a robotic MIG welding cell with an L-shaped structural chassis bracket. To weld both perpendicular seams, the robot arm was forced into awkward vertical-down positions, causing severe spatter, cold laps, and high rework. Loading and manual clamping took 95 seconds per bracket.

The CyTOS Engineering Solution: CyTOS engineered a custom pneumatic fixture with hardened tool-steel datum blocks and pneumatic toggle clamps. The entire clamping bed is mounted on a heavy-duty 90-degree rotary actuator. After the first seam is welded in a flat horizontal position, the fixture rotates 90° so the second seam is also welded in the exact same optimal flat downhand orientation.

CASE #03 • HIGH-VOLUME PCB FABRICATION

3× Throughput Expansion with 3-Spindle Synchronized PCB Drilling

The Initial Bottleneck: A high-volume PCB fabrication house was running 3 shifts at 100% capacity on legacy single-spindle drilling machines. Drilling dense telecommunications backplanes with over 12,000 micro-vias (0.25mm) per panel was causing severe delivery backlogs and frequent broken micro-bits due to spindle vibration.

The CyTOS Engineering Solution: CyTOS deployed the PCB12 multi-spindle drilling system. Featuring a 1,200 × 1,200 mm ground granite base, three independently calibrated 60,000 RPM high-frequency spindles, and laser tool-breakage detection, the machine drills three full panels concurrently with synchronized servo pitch adjustment.

Same-Day PCB R&D Iteration for Automotive Telematics Design Group
