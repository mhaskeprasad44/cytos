Commercial Machinery Supply Terms & Conditions

Standard contractual specifications, Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT), site readiness requirements, 12-month equipment warranty, and legal jurisdiction for capital machine tools manufactured by Cybernetic Technologies & Operating Systems (CyTOS) in Pune, Maharashtra.

Table of Contents

01. Scope of Quotations & Contract Formation

All commercial proposals, technical quotations, and machinery proforma invoices issued by Cybernetic Technologies & Operating Systems (hereinafter referred to as "CyTOS") are valid for a period of thirty (30) calendar days from the date of issuance, unless explicitly stated otherwise in writing.

A legally binding purchase contract is formed upon written acceptance of the Buyer's formal Purchase Order (PO) accompanied by the contractual advance payment. Technical specifications, machine layout drawings, and cycle-time projections detailed in CyTOS formal quotations form an integral schedule of the supply agreement.

Taxation & Statutory Levies

All prices quoted are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax (GST) applicable under Indian law (presently 18% on capital machine tools under HSN 8459 / 8465), customs duties for export orders, and local octroi/entry tolls, which shall be charged at actuals at the time of invoicing.

02. Commercial Payment Terms & Milestone Schedules

Unless negotiated and explicitly documented in a mutually executed Master Supply Agreement, CyTOS standard commercial milestone terms for standard and custom machinery are structured as follows:

  • Initial Advance Payment: 40% of the total PO contract value payable along with the Purchase Order to initiate component procurement, raw material allocation, and machine fabrication.
  • Pre-Dispatch Milestone: 50% payable upon successful completion and joint sign-off of the Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) at the CyTOS Pune manufacturing works, prior to dispatch.
  • Final Commissioning Balance: 10% payable within fifteen (15) days of successful Site Acceptance Testing (SAT) and commissioning at the Buyer's facility, or thirty (30) days from the date of machine delivery, whichever occurs first.

For export shipments, payments are accepted via 100% Irrevocable Letter of Credit (LC) at sight opened through a premier scheduled commercial bank, or direct Wire Transfer (SWIFT) per agreed milestone schedules.

03. Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) & Pre-Dispatch Inspection

Prior to shipping any CNC machine, PCB drilling center, or turnkey SPM, CyTOS conducts comprehensive pre-dispatch testing at its Pune facility:

  • Laser Interferometer & Dial Gauge Metrology: Verification of positional repeatability (±10µm), linear accuracy, and perpendicularity across all axes (X, Y, Z).
  • Spindle Dynamic Balancing & Runout Audit: Verification of dynamic TIR (<3µm) across the full operating RPM band (up to 60,000 RPM for air/ceramic bearing spindles).
  • Sample Cutting / Job Trials: Test cutting of customer-supplied raw material (e.g., FR4 blanks, aluminum alloy plates, acrylic panels, or automotive brackets) against customer CAD/CAM/Gerber files.

The Buyer’s designated technical engineers are welcome to attend the FAT in Pune. Upon completion, a formal FAT Clearance Certificate is jointly executed.

04. Packaging, Transit Logistics & Insurance

All equipment is packaged in export-grade treated wooden crates with Vapor Corrosion Inhibitor (VCI) poly-sheeting, desiccants, and heavy gantry axis immobilization brackets to prevent linear bearing, ball screw, or spindle damage during transport.

Standard delivery terms are Ex-Works CyTOS Plant (Pune, India) or F.O.R. Destination as agreed in the quotation. Transit insurance from CyTOS works to the Buyer’s unloading bay must be secured by the Buyer, unless explicitly included as a line-item in CyTOS's commercial invoice.

05. Customer Site Readiness & Commissioning (SAT)

Successful machine installation and Site Acceptance Testing (SAT) depend on proper facility readiness by the Buyer before CyTOS service engineers arrive:

  • Pneumatic Supply: Continuous clean, dry, oil-free compressed air at 6.0 to 8.0 bar pressure with dedicated refrigerated air dryer and 5µm particulate filters.
  • Electrical Power: 3-Phase 415V AC ±5%, 50Hz stabilized industrial power supply with neutral-to-earth voltage strictly maintained below 3.0V. CyTOS strongly advises a dedicated Servo Voltage Stabilizer.
  • Civil Foundation: Reinforced concrete floor (minimum 150mm thick M20 grade RCC) free from heavy external punch-press or forge vibrations.
  • Ambient Conditions: Ambient operating temperature between 18°C and 32°C with non-condensing relative humidity below 75%.

06. Standard 12-Month Equipment Warranty

CyTOS provides a comprehensive twelve (12) month manufacturer warranty covering all mechanical assemblies, linear motion guides, ball screws, servo drives, and structural fabricated components from the date of successful commissioning, or fifteen (15) months from the date of dispatch, whichever is earlier.

Warranty Coverage & Exclusions

Covered: Free replacement or factory repair of parts found defective due to faulty materials or CyTOS manufacturing workmanship.

Excluded: Normal wear-and-tear consumables (collets, router bits, micro-drills, vacuum pump vanes), machine crashes caused by erroneous operator G-code, unauthorized spindle disassembly, contaminated compressed air supply, or input voltage spikes exceeding OEM tolerances.

07. Spare Parts Commitment & Annual Maintenance (AMC)

CyTOS guarantees the availability of original replacement components, drive belts, ball screws, servo motors, and controller cards for a minimum of seven (7) years following machine delivery.

Post-warranty, customers may enroll in CyTOS Comprehensive or Non-Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), guaranteeing quarterly preventive maintenance audits, priority engineering dispatch (within 24–48 hours across western India), and discounted spare parts.

08. Controller Software, Firmware & Intellectual Property

All proprietary PLC ladder logic, macro software routines, HMI graphical interface layouts, and machine firmware developed by CyTOS remain the exclusive intellectual property of CyTOS.

The Buyer is granted a perpetual, non-transferable, royalty-free operating license to utilize the installed software solely for operating the specific machine serial number supplied. Reverse engineering, decompiling, or cloning CyTOS proprietary automation sequences is strictly prohibited.

09. Limitation of Liability & Force Majeure

In no event shall CyTOS, its directors, or engineering personnel be liable to the Buyer for indirect, incidental, punitive, or consequential damages, including loss of production, raw material spoilage, or commercial business interruption, regardless of whether CyTOS was advised of the possibility of such damages. CyTOS maximum aggregate liability under any purchase order shall not exceed the net capital amount received by CyTOS for the specific machine unit in question.

Neither party shall be held liable for delivery delays resulting from acts of God, extreme natural disasters, pandemic lockdowns, civil unrest, statutory import embargoes, or transportation strikes beyond reasonable commercial control.

10. Governing Law & Pune Legal Jurisdiction

All commercial transactions, quotation agreements, purchase orders, and disputes arising out of or related to machinery supply contracts with CyTOS shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the substantive laws of the Republic of India.

Any legal dispute, suit, or claim that cannot be amicably settled through mutual executive conciliation within thirty (30) days shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of law in Pune, Maharashtra, India.
