Material & Industrial Applications Guide

Every material has unique chip formation dynamics, thermal conductivity, and tooling requirements. This guide provides factory-tested parameters for FR4 circuit boards, aluminium MCPCBs, aerospace alloys, acrylics, and structural welding automation.

In brief: CyTOS CNC machines and automation systems are calibrated for specific substrate behaviors. From micro-drilling FR4 boards at 60,000 RPM without copper burrs, to cutting aerospace 6061 aluminium with polished single-flute end mills and alcohol-mist cooling, our Pune engineers assist you in choosing the exact machine model, spindle power, and tooling package for zero trial-and-error waste.
