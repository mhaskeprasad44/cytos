CLAUSE 02

Proprietary Engineering Drawings & CAD/Gerber Confidentiality

CyTOS recognizes that files submitted by prospective and existing clients—including PCB Gerber RS-274X archives, Excellon drill files, ODB++ packages, AutoCAD DXF/DWG part drawings, SolidWorks models, and STEP/IGES assemblies—represent mission-critical intellectual property and commercial trade secrets.

Strict Non-Disclosure

Customer engineering files are treated as Strictly Confidential. They are accessible exclusively by authorized CyTOS application engineers for cycle-time calculations, toolpath simulation, and fixture engineering.

No Reverse-Engineering

CyTOS strictly guarantees that no customer part geometries, PCB circuit tracks, or mechanical designs will ever be reverse-engineered, shared with third parties, or replicated for competing clients.

Upon written request or completion of the feasibility evaluation without machine procurement, all customer drawings and files will be permanently purged from our internal servers.

CLAUSE 03

Personal & Commercial Data Collected

We collect only the minimum necessary data required to engineer machine systems, prepare customized commercial quotations, and execute statutory compliance:

  • Contact Identifiers: Name, professional title, corporate designation, email address, mobile/WhatsApp telephone number.
  • Corporate & Billing Data: Company legal name, factory delivery address, Goods & Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN), and State Jurisdiction code.
  • Machine Application Requirements: Intended workpiece material (FR4, Aluminium, Brass, Acrylic, Composites), panel thickness, tolerance parameters (±0.01mm to ±0.05mm), spindle speed requirements, and estimated monthly production batch volumes.
  • Digital Telemetry: Anonymized IP addresses, browser specifications, and referral sources collected via server access logs for cybersecurity monitoring and load balancing.
CLAUSE 04

Lawful Purpose of Data Processing

In strict accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India), your data is processed solely under the following lawful bases:

  1. Contractual Performance: Providing machine tool quotations, bill of materials (BOM), conducting Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT), coordinating site transit, and honoring the 12-month comprehensive warranty.
  2. Engineering Feasibility: Calculating spindle cycle time, tool wear rates, and pneumatic clamping kinematics for custom SPMs.
  3. Statutory & Tax Obligations: Generating e-Way bills, tax invoices, and statutory audit filings under Indian GST regulations.
  4. Operational Support: Providing direct telephonic and on-site engineering assistance, spare parts dispatch, and software updates for CyTOS CNC Studio.

Industrial Cybersecurity & Encryption Standards

  • Physical Security: CyTOS Pune works maintains 24×7 physical security and controlled access to assembly bays and testing rigs.

CLAUSE 09

Client Data Rights under DPDP Act 2023

As a data principal under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you retain the following statutory rights regarding your personal and corporate information:

  • Right of Access & Summary: Request an itemized summary of all personal and company data held by CyTOS.
  • Right of Rectification: Update inaccurate billing addresses, GST details, or designated technical contact persons.
  • Right of Erasure: Request the deletion of non-statutory data, CAD files, and marketing contact details.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke prior consent for marketing communications or technical newsletter mailings at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, submit a written communication to our Data Grievance Officer at info@cytos.in.
